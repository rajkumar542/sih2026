1.Who are we trying to help? Elderly people, specifically those suffering from dementia 

2.Where are we focusing? The North Eastern Region (NER) of India.

3.What is the core issue? more of them(elderly people) are developing memory issues. However, many live in remote or rural areas where it is very hard to find doctors, specialized therapists, or support centers.

the effect:

-> For the Elderly: They feel confused, forget things easily, get anxious, and often feel lonely.

-> For the Caregivers (Family/Nurses): It is incredibly stressful to constantly monitor and keep the elderly person engaged in a healthy way.

-> The Gap: There aren't many digital tools (like apps or games) that are affordable, easy to use, and most importantly designed to feel "local" and culturally familiar to the people of the North East.

The core idea : 

This problem statement seeks the development of an AI-powered cognitive gaming and memory assistance platform for elderly dementia patients in the North Eastern Region.

solution mainly focus on :

A: Memory Improvement,Attention and Concentration,Daily Routine Recall,Pattern and Object Recognition,Emotional and Mental Engagement

->games that help the person practice remembering short-term and long-term things.

->Dementia makes it hard to focus on one thing for very long. These games train the brain to stay focused and avoid getting distracted.

->helping them remember the basic steps to get through their normal day. Dementia often makes people forget how to do simple tasks.

->helps them practice identifying the world around them.

->They need to keep the person's mind active while making them feel happy, calm, or entertained.

B: Use AI/ML algorithms to adapt difficulty levels based on patient performance and cognitive condition 

->The app must automatically adjust how hard the games are while the person is playing.

->If they are struggling: The AI notices they are getting answers wrong or taking too long, and instantly makes the game easier so they don't get frustrated or upset.

->If they are doing well: The AI notices they are finding it easy, and slightly increases the challenge so their brain gets a good workout.

C:Support multilingual and voice-assisted interaction suitable for elderly users in NER Include culturally familiar themes, visuals, sounds, and regional language support for 

D: better engagement

E: Provide reminders for:
• Medicines
• Hydration
• Daily activities
• Medical appointments.

F: Enable caregivers and healthcare workers to monitor patient progress through dashboards and activity levels

G: Work in low-connectivity environments with offline functionality support

H: Be accessible through mobile/tablet devices with a simple and elderly-friendly interface The platform should encourage long-term cognitive engagement, emotional well-being, and social interaction among elderly users.

Expected Solution: A user-friendly Al-enabled cognitive assistance platform with:

• Adaptive gaming and memory training modules
• Voice-enabled multilingual interface
• Cognitive performance tracking and analytics dashboard
• Caregiver monitoring and alert system
• Offline synchronization support for remote areas
• Secure patient data management system
• Simple and accessible UI/UX designed for elderly users The solution should support early cognitive intervention, improve quality of life for elderly dementia patients, and strengthen digital healthcare accessibility across the North Eastern Region.
